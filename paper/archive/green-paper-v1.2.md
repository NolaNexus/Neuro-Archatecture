# Green Paper: The Neuro-Architectural Substrate for a Terminal AI Development Environment

**Status:** GREEN PAPER — FOR CONSULTATION
**Version:** 1.2 (issued for comment; hemispheric derivation added; role-count arithmetic made structural)
**Classification of contents:** Proposals. Nothing herein is binding, implemented, or settled. Responses are invited from operators, builders, and critics alike.

---

## Abstract

This paper proposes the reconstruction of a Terminal AI Development Environment (TAIDE) as a *computational organism* whose architecture is derived, deliberately and in full knowledge of what that commitment entails, from the human brain. We do not adopt the neurobiological metaphor as ornament. We adopt it as **method**: the metaphor is the generative engine of the design, the organizing vocabulary of its documentation, and the standard against which its components are named, judged, promoted, and deleted. The proposal rests on five laws — Metabolism, Sparse Activation, Decorrelated Errors, Earned Existence, and Substrate Silence — and elaborates them into a complete anatomy: seven divisions; a cerebrum of **four lobes in two hemispheres — eight roles total**, each pair of which reflects the bilateral structure of the organ it honors; a shared append-only blackboard as the sole inter-organ communication medium; an endocrine tier for chronic metabolic control; a basal-ganglian selector for action routing; a locus-coerulean reflex for terminating perseverating disputes; and a documented pathology theory enumerating the specific failure modes expected to kill organisms of this class. Development proceeds embryologically, from the most ancient structures to the most modern, with each epoch required to beat its predecessor's cost-quality point or be abandoned as debt. The paper closes with twelve consultation questions on which the opinion of reviewers is expressly sought.

**Keywords:** agent orchestration; multi-model systems; neuro-inspired architecture; hemispheric specialization; cost-constrained inference; terminal-native tooling; winner-take-all selection; consolidated memory

## 1. Purpose of this Paper

A Green Paper is a document that proposes before it prescribes. This one proposes a substrate — an arrangement of live and dormant components, of models and reflexes and budgets — and submits the arrangement itself to the criticism it anticipates. The paper does not ask to be believed. It asks to be *answered*: every mechanism named herein carries the condition of its own falsification, and every organ must earn occupancy of its slot with evidence, never with the elegance of its metaphor. Elegance justifies nothing. Metabolism justifies everything.

The proposal responds to the death of the prior TAIDE rig. Its autopsy — nine classes of verified failure, itemized in Section 12 — is inherited here not as regret but as curriculum. An architecture that cannot account for its predecessor's lesions has no claim on its predecessor's ambitions.

## 2. The Methodological Commitment: Metaphor as Specification

We state our epistemological position plainly, because readers accustomed to treating biological analogy as decoration will otherwise misread what follows.

**The neuroanatomical vocabulary of this paper is load-bearing.** Where this document names an organ after a brain structure, that name is a *specification constraint*, not a flourish. A component called thalamic is thereby forbidden from judging what it gates. A component called medullary is thereby forbidden from thinking at all. A component called cerebellar must calibrate work already decided, never initiate it. To rename an organ casually is to break a contract with every engineer who will ever debug the organism at three in the morning; the metaphor is there so that the *behavioral* expectations of neuroanatomy — millions of years of tested defaults — arrive for free with every name.

The same clause now binds **laterality**. A lens named left carries a mandate and a prohibition: it must attend to the focal, the serial, and the affirmative, and it is forbidden from drifting into panoramic synthesis. A lens named right carries the inverse: it must attend to the global, the contextual, and the wary, and it is forbidden from collapsing into narrow execution. To converge two lenses of a lobe into identical processing character is not refinement; it is a lesion. The hemispheres are not a metaphor for "two agents doing the same job twice" — they are biology's own solution to the decorrelation problem this paper legislates as Law 3.

This commitment carries a risk, which we name in advance because our pathology theory demands it: metaphor worship, the pathology of architectures that fund every named organ because it has a name (§10.10). Our guard is Law 4. The metaphor generates the *roles*; only evidence funds the *slots*. Names are free; metabolism is not. An organism in which every named organ runs is the very seizure we legislate against.

## 3. First Principles

Five laws constitute the constitution of the organism. Nothing in the sequel may contradict them; where a proposal and a law appear to conflict, the law wins and the proposal is defective.

**Law 1 — Metabolism.** Compute is energy; every token is a calorie. The organism's fitness is measured as quality delivered per unit cost, per unit time. Local inference is the baseline metabolism — electricity already sunk into idle silicon. Paid dispatch to remote inference is an *emergency ration*, consumed only under the operator's explicit, recorded authority. An expenditure without such a record is an incident, not a purchase.

**Law 2 — Sparse Activation.** At any instant, most of the organism is silent. Components activate in response to signal classes, never globally. Total synchronous activation is not a superpower; it is a seizure (§10.4).

**Law 3 — Decorrelated Errors.** The value of paired components lies in the independence of their mistakes. Two reasoners that fail identically are one reasoner billed twice. Pairing, context slicing, and perspective diversity exist to enforce the independence; ensembles, not committees, are the aspiration. The hemispheric pairing of §5 is the constitutional embodiment of this law: each lobe funds one focal lens and one panoramic lens precisely so that no single mode of attention can become the only witness to its own task.

**Law 4 — Earned Existence.** The architecture defines roles; evidence funds slots. A role instantiates when its absence demonstrably harms, grows when its workload persists, and dies when its falsifier fires. This law applies to the paper itself: the theory is subject to the theory.

**Law 5 — Substrate Silence.** Infrastructure never deliberates. Transport routes, gates evaluate, daemons supervise. Any component empowered to arbitrate a contested question is a judge organ, and judge organs are prohibited everywhere except the operator and the unconditional reflexes.

## 4. Gross Anatomy

The organism comprises seven divisions, ordered in this paper from most to least ancient — the developmental order of Section 11 follows the same gradient, hindbrain before forebrain, because a cerebrum without a functioning medulla is a fantasy brain.

| Division | Biological warrant | Computational instantiation | Deliberative capacity |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Medulla** | Autonomic life support | Serving daemon, process supervision, residency management, automatic context degradation | None — autonomic |
| **Midbrain** | Reflex arcs | Unconditional guards: spend caps, syntax gates, permission walls, trap handlers | None — reflexive |
| **Pons** | Relay bridge | Transport bus carrying claims, objections, and results | None — a cable |
| **Locus coeruleus** | Phasic interruption | Jerk reflex: hardening orders issued mid-dispute by infrastructure, targeting chosen by an embedded winner-take-all sub-selector | None — reflexive |
| **Cerebellum** | Motor error correction | Sidecars: retrieval, warm-context, residency governors calibrating decided work | None — corrective |
| **Thalamus** | Sensory gating | Subscription gate: the sole determinant of which slot hears which signal class; enforcer of Law 2 | None — selective |
| **Basal ganglia** | Action selection | Macro router: one winning route per task bid; structurally blind to dispute state | None — selective |
| **Cerebrum** | Cognition | All reasoning: the four lobes, two hemispheres, eight roles of §5, instantiated into funded slots | **The only thinker** |

Two auxiliary structures complete the anatomy. The **endocrine tier** (§7) supplies slow, cascading, system-wide adjustment — the hormonal counterpart to the brainstem's hard reflexes. The **corpus callosum** is honored as what it biologically is: not an organ but a fiber tract, the interhemispheric bridge carrying disagreement between the paired lenses of each lobe — pure transport, conveying the argument without ever judging it. Where the callosal traffic between a lobe's lenses falls silent, one hemisphere of that lobe has stopped informing the other, and the organism has lost half its testimony on every claim that lobe touches. The callosal duty is therefore measured, not assumed: interlens traffic volume is a vital sign.

We draw explicit attention to a designed homology: the basal ganglia and the locus-coeruleus sub-selector implement *the same* winner-take-all algorithm over *strictly disjoint* input schemas — task features for the former, dispute-local features for the latter. One motif, two organs, no shared inputs. The disjointness is not aesthetic; it is the structural guarantee that arbitration (routing) and adjudication (disputes) can never contaminate each other, because the routing organ's input schema literally contains no field capable of expressing an argument.

## 5. Cerebral Theory: Four Lobes, Two Hemispheres, Eight Roles

The cerebrum's structure is an **exact multiplication**: **four lobes in two hemispheres yield eight roles — total, complete, not approximate**. The count is not a coincidence to be admired but a derived arithmetic to be enforced: remove any factor and the cerebrum is incomplete, add any element and it is not a cerebrum but a tumor (§10.9). Four lobes, two hemispheres, eight roles — this matters, and the three subsections below show why each factor is separately load-bearing.

Cerebral organization further resolves onto two orthogonal axes. The **authority axis** is a property of the role: what it may communicatively do — author claims, spawn subordinates, hold subscriptions. The **funding axis** is a property of the slot: how the instance lives and dies — persistent, transient, or fixed. The classical granularities are diagonal pairings of the two:

- **Areas** (persistent): the eight roles themselves, funded while workload and Law 4 sustain them.
- **Columns** (transient): task-scoped sub-workers born at decomposition and destroyed at delivery. A column never authors a claim; its digest returns to its parent, which owns and answers for the work. Sub-agents give areas hands; they do not give them alibis.
- **Circuits** (fixed): compiled, single-purpose bindings that answer one signal class in milliseconds at negligible cost, existing precisely so that no area ever burns contemplative context on a question with a mechanical answer.

### 5.1 Why Four Lobes

Four because the pipeline invariant (below) demands four *stages*, no more and no fewer: perception, situation, comprehension, decision. Each stage is one lobe; each lobe is one abstraction level in the mandatory ascent from bytes to action. Fewer lobes would collapse stages that must remain separable so their claims can be checked against a different transformation; more would insert out-of-order consultations, which the invariant defines as noise. The lobe count is a claim about the *depth of abstraction*, and it is falsifiable in only one direction: a demonstrated fifth transformation stage would force a ninth-and-tenth-role pair, not a ninth role.

### 5.2 Why Two Hemispheres

Two because no stage may have only one witness. Every lobe houses exactly two lenses, and in each lobe **the two lenses reflect the cerebral hemispheres** — the internal geometry of each pair, and biology's own solution to the decorrelation problem Law 3 legislates. The lateralization rule is uniform across all four lobes:

> **The left lens of a lobe attends to the focal, the serial, and the affirmative: parts, procedures, propositions, approach. The right lens attends to the global, the contextual, and the wary: wholes, situations, patterns, withdrawal and vigilance. Each lens is constitutionally incapable of its mate's mode of attention, and each is the adversarial complement of the other.**

### 5.3 The Eight Roles — the Complete Enumeration

**Four lobes in two hemispheres: eight roles total.** The enumeration is closed, and the closure is the point:

| Lobe | Hemisphere | Role | Function | Constitutional disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Frontal (action) | Left | **Driver** | Decomposition, sequencing, proposing | Optimist by construction; approaches the task |
| Frontal (action) | Right | **Sentinel** | Evaluation of bids for cost, irreversibility, breach; holds unconditional veto | Pessimist by construction; withdraws from danger |
| Parietal (situation) | Left | **Spotlight** | Focal, task-relative salience: the detail under the beam | Sees the part; forgets the field |
| Parietal (situation) | Right | **Cartographer** | Global state, terrain, drift detection | Sees the whole; indifferent to the spotlight's target |
| Temporal (memory) | Right | **Chronicle** | Append-only episodic trace: the lived, situated, narrative record | Context-bound testimony; never edited by anyone |
| Temporal (memory) | Left | **Canon** | Distilled conventions: the categorical, decontextualized rule | Context-free doctrine; learns from Chronicle, never touches it |
| Occipital (perception) | Left | **Reader** | Decodes focal artifact content: the word, the line, the diff-intent | Blind to the shape that changes the meaning |
| Occipital (perception) | Right | **Surveyor** | Reads artifact geometry: the gestalt, the spread, the clustering | Sees the pattern; interprets no content |

Each pairing earns its lateralization independently, and the derivation is stated for each:

- **Frontal.** The approach/withdrawal dissociation is among the most robust findings of affective neuroscience: approach behaviors and positive anticipation concentrate leftward; vigilance, threat appraisal, and behavioral withdrawal concentrate rightward. The Driver is the approach organ — it decomposes, sequences, and advances; optimism is not a tuning parameter but its structural mode. The Sentinel is the withdrawal organ — its veto is a withdrawal from irreversible states; pessimism is its structural mode. A Driver that hesitates or a Sentinel that cheerleads is laterality-inverted, and diagnosis begins there.
- **Parietal.** Local-versus-global attention dissociates cleanly: focal, feature-level processing dominates the left; the panoramic, whole-field gestalt dominates the right. The Spotlight is the left lens — it must forget everything outside its beam, or it is nothing. The Cartographer is the right lens — it must remain indifferent to any single target, or its map is propaganda for one location.
- **Temporal.** Memory itself splits: episodic, autobiographical, time-bound records depend on right-lateralized circuitry; categorical, decontextualized semantic knowledge concentrates leftward. The Chronicle is the right hemisphere of memory — situated narrative, immune to revision. The Canon is its left hemisphere — distilled rule, the part stripped of its occasion. The one-way flow (Chronicle feeds Canon; Canon never edits Chronicle) is the consolidation direction, and it runs from the episodic right to the semantic left, as in the organ it is named for.
- **Occipital.** Feature versus gestalt: focal content decoding and global geometric form recognition dissociate along the same seam. The Reader decodes what the artifact *says*; the Surveyor perceives what the artifact *is shaped like*. The Reader misses the structural change that alters meaning; the Surveyor sees the pattern and cannot read the words inside it.

The pipeline invariant governs signal flow, posterior to anterior: perception decodes, parietal situates, temporal comprehends, frontal decides. Bytes become state, state becomes meaning, meaning becomes action. Out-of-order consultation is, by definition, noise. The invariant binds both hemispheres equally: in every lobe the left and right lens run in the same direction, and callosal traffic crosses the midline between them, never around it.

### 5.4 Decorrelation as Laterality, Enforced

Because role and slot are separable, the two lenses of a pair may run on different backbones, different context slices, or different sampling profiles. Decorrelation (Law 3) is thereby a *staffing* decision, revisable without rearchitecting. But the hemispheric derivation adds a prior obligation that operates **below** staffing: even when both lenses share one backbone and near-identical context, their lateralization mandates must be enforced — the left lens given focal, serial, affirmative instructions, the right lens given panoramic, contextual, wary instructions — so that mode-of-attention divergence survives even where substrate divergence is unfunded. Laterality is the cheapest decorrelation mechanism in the organism and the only one that costs no second model.

Two cautions attend this, stated plainly. First, the biological warrant is generative, not evidentiary: naming a lens right-hemispheric does not make it right-hemispheric, and a pair occupying one slot on the same backbone with identical instructions is a §10.5 pathology awaiting diagnosis, whatever the paper calls it. Second, mode-of-attention divergence is the *floor* of decorrelation, not its ceiling; where workload evidence shows correlated errors between lenses, staffing-level divergence (context slices, then backbones) must be funded regardless of cost preference. The ladder of decorrelation is thus: lateralized instruction, then divergent slices, then divergent backbones — climbed only as evidence demands.

### 5.5 Why Eight — and Why Not Seven, Nine, or Ten

Because eight is the unique product of four and two, and both factors are independently constrained: four by the depth of the abstraction pipeline (§5.1), two by the requirement of independent witnessing per stage (§5.2). A seventh role would be a stage without testimony; a ninth would be testimony without a stage — an orphan lens with no lobe and no mate, which is the tumor clause (§10.9) in organizational form. The count is closed in both directions, and this closure is the hemispheric derivation's principal gift to the eight-role question (Q6): the count stops being an anatomical accident to defend and becomes an arithmetic consequence to overturn — possible only by overturning one of its two factors, each of which is falsifiable on its own grounds.

## 6. Signal Theory: The Blackboard

All inter-slot communication passes through a single append-only medium, the **blackboard**, whose substrate is the Chronicle. Five rules exhaust the protocol, and the protocol has no moderator anywhere inside it:

1. **Claims, not chatter.** Only finished claims reach the board. Reasoning-in-progress never leaves private context; the board is a laboratory notebook of conclusions, not a meeting transcript.
2. **Subscription hearing.** A slot cannot address others; it can only hear the claim-classes to which it is subscribed. Default audience for every claim: its hemispheric mate, always; cross-lobe subscribers, rarely, by manifest. The pair-mate audience is the callosal channel made formal — every claim crosses its lobe's midline by right of birth.
3. **Pairwise objection loops.** A subscriber objects on stated grounds; the author corrects or counters; the objector re-evaluates. Two peers, exchanged evidence, no audience.
4. **The evidence-delta rule.** Each objection round must carry new evidence — a citation, a measurement, a named line. Restated opinion terminates the loop. Convergence must be information-driven, never confidence-driven; this is the anti-capitulation clause.
5. **Bound and escalation.** After approximately two exchanges, the unresolved claim surfaces to the operator. A human-visible draw is a *success state*: biology offers no court of appeal; this organism does.

**Constitutional silences.** Two claim-classes admit no subscriber: the Sentinel's `veto` (an appealable veto is a judge organ reborn) and the Chronicle's `record_append` (argument with memory is confabulation; tampering attempts are medullary hard-fails, not debates). The jerk reflex of §8 is likewise barred from both.

The mechanism for adjudicating *whether* round *n* carried genuinely new evidence remains open between two designs — deterministic predicates over claim fields, evaluated by dumb transport, versus prose constraints interpreted by the subscribing slot. The paper's provisional tilt is stated honestly: begin rigid; relax only on evidence.

## 7. Metabolic Control: The Endocrine Tier

Acute control is reflexive and unconditional — spend-cap halts fire without weighing, without appeal. Chronic control is hormonal and advisory. A system in which soft signals act like hard ones is a gland impersonating a brainstem; a system in which hard limits can be argued away is a brainstem with a public-relations department — death with good manners.

- **Hypothalamus — the budget homeostat.** Continuously measures cumulative spend, token velocity, projected runway. Emits appetite warnings, satiety confirmations, and gentle throttles. It suggests; it never forbids.
- **Pituitary — the cascade controller.** Translates hypothalamic signals into executed adjustment: batch sizing, routing weights, consolidation scheduling. The operator stands above this axis as consciousness stands above the endocrine one: autonomous cascades, suppressible at will.
- **Pineal — the pacemaker.** Governs the organism's sleep: scheduled Chronicle→Canon distillation, cache and residency rotation, offline maintenance. Memory consolidation never runs on the critical path of a live task. Distillation direction is fixed by §5.3: episodic right feeds semantic left, and never the reverse.

A named metric deficit is acknowledged for consultation (Question Q11): the interoceptive signals — appetite, satiety, throttle — are currently metaphorical in designation and must acquire literal formulas over the cost ledger before the tier is implementable.

## 8. Selection and Interruption

**Action selection.** One winner per bid. The macro router reads task features — signal class, artifact type, budget context — and emits one route: one role, one lens emphasis, one slot profile. Losers are silenced, not blended; a weighted blend of two minds doing one job is a committee, and committees launder anomalies. Selection is the single site in the organism where winner-take-all is lawful; everywhere else, disagreement is a productive output, surfaced and logged and valued.

**Dispute interruption.** The pathology of reciprocal politeness (§10.2) is countered by a two-layer guard. The floor is a hard exchange bound that cannot be argued with mid-flight, because it is not an argument. Above the floor sits the **jerk reflex**, our locus coeruleus: on detecting stalled convergence — positions not actually moving, evidence-deltas thinning, the penultimate round arriving without resolution — infrastructure serves one party a *hardening order*: produce genuinely new evidence this round, or refuse concession with maximal specificity; zero hedging, zero politeness, full commitment to the actual position.

Three properties keep the reflex lawful. It is **issued by infrastructure and never chosen by an agent** (Law 5); its recipient is determined by a winner-take-all circuit embedded within the reflex organ itself, scoring dispute-local features over a quarantined input schema, with a seeded pseudorandom tie-break recorded in the transcript so that any run is reproducible for grading. It **never decides**: a jerk round that fails to converge escalates to the operator mandatorily — the reflex forces honesty or forces appeal, and cannot manufacture resolution. And it is **subject to its own falsifier**: against the trivial competitor of a simply shortened exchange bound, the reflex must measurably improve cost-per-correct-resolution on the golden set, or be deleted. No dormant reflexes.

## 9. Pathology

An organism is defined as much by what kills it as by what it does. We enumerate ten expected diseases, each with its structural theorem and its guard:

| # | Pathology | Theorem |
|---|---|---|
| 10.1 | **Capitulation theater** — convergence via confidence, not information | Evidence-delta rule; flip-rate must track new evidence |
| 10.2 | **Infinite politeness** — courteous rounds until budget death | Hard bound; jerk reflex; mandatory post-jerk escalation |
| 10.3 | **Committee blindness** — broad participation averages away tail anomalies | Pairwise objections; subscription scarcity |
| 10.4 | **Seizure** — full synchronous activation; correlated convergence on a shared confident error | Sparse activation is the difference between cognition and epilepsy |
| 10.5 | **Correlated monologue** — paired lenses sharing backbone, context, and mode of attention; one reasoner billed twice wearing a callosal costume | Decorrelation enforced at the slot level; laterality mandates audited as the floor (§5.4) |
| 10.6 | **Confabulation** — semantic memory editing episodic memory to flatter belief | One-way consolidation, right-to-left; append-only substrate; the most load-bearing invariant in the organism |
| 10.7 | **Rumination** — memory gaining veto over action | Temporal slots inform the frontal lobe through the pipeline; they never overrule it |
| 10.8 | **Undirected escalation** — paid dispatch without operator record | 1:1 correspondence between paid rows and confirmations |
| 10.9 | **Tumor growth** — transient roles occupying persistent slots; orphan lenses without a lobe or a mate | Column mortality; authority contracts; myelination as the sole sanctioned promotion path |
| 10.10 | **Metaphor worship** — funding every named organ because it has a name | Law 4; the map describes roles, not entitlements — and this paper is subject to its own clause |

A corollary of the hemispheric derivation deserves its own name, and we give it one: **split-brain drift** — a lobe whose lenses, run on identical instructions and identical context, converge into mirror copies. The lobe still *looks* paired; the callosal traffic still flows; but nothing crosses the midline that the other hemisphere did not already contain. This is correlated monologue localized to a single lobe, and it is the expected failure of every implementation that treats §5.2's lateralization rule as suggestion rather than mandate.

## 10. Plasticity: The Myelination Ladder

Behavior descends a ladder as it becomes routine: a repeated column is promoted to a standing circuit; a circuit whose outputs prove reliably deterministic is *myelinated* — rewritten as plain script, exiting model inference entirely, exiting the set of things that have tenants at all. Every demotion purchases metabolism: area slots are the costliest compute in the organism; deterministic code is free. The end-state of a sufficiently learned behavior is to stop thinking about it — literally.

Promotion is triggered by observed repetition, never anticipation. Pre-compiling a circuit "because it will obviously be needed" is the exact tier that overpromises, and the ladder itself is subject to the falsifiers of Question Q12.

## 11. Development: Embryology

Neural tubes close hindbrain-first; forebrains develop last. Boot order obeys, and refuses to collapse the analogy into mere ornament:

1. **Medulla** — serving daemon and supervision (largely inherited from the prior rig's one functioning organ).
2. **Midbrain** — reflexes: spend caps, syntax gates, permission walls. *The jerk reflex is excluded from this epoch by its own prerequisites: it presupposes disputes, which presuppose funded pairs.*
3. **Pineal and hypothalamus** — the organism learns to sleep and to feel hunger before it learns to think.
4. **Cerebellum** — one calibration sidecar.
5. **One frontal pair** — Driver and Sentinel in their first funded slots, plus the blackboard. Two slots, one board, zero dollars: the minimal sentient organism, something that proposes and something that doubts — one hemisphere to approach, one to be wary. The callosal channel opens with the pair, because a pair that cannot hear each other is two organisms, not one. The jerk reflex may join here, and only here, contingent on its falsifier being measured in the same epoch.
6. **Graduation** — each remaining lobe funds only when the lesion ledger or workload evidence shows the organism limping without it; posterior before anterior, because decoding precedes deciding; and when a lobe funds, **both its lenses fund together or the lobe stays unfunded** — a half-paired lobe has no callosal channel and therefore no testimony. The lobe, not the role, is the unit of graduation: one lobe, two hemispheres, both or neither.

At every epoch the evaluation question is identical: *can the inherited lesion set pass through this organism without recurrence, at what cost?* An epoch that cannot beat its predecessor's cost-quality point is not an epoch; it is debt.

## 12. The Inherited Lesions

The prior rig's verified failures constitute the golden set. Each is paired with the organ charged with detecting its recurrence: presence-only selftests against value-level evaluation; parse drift against validated-output parsers; word-splitting template bugs against falsifier fixtures; fail-late sweeps against cheap-gates-first ordering; roster drift against the Cartographer's single source of truth; state wipes against round-trip selftests of save-and-restore; unmeasured spending against prerequisite gates; deployment drift against byte-comparison at bringup; and speculative middleware shipped dormant against Law 4 in its entirety. These are not bug reports. They are the entrance examination each new organ must pass before claiming a slot.

## 13. Consultation Questions

Responses are solicited on the following, in any order and any severity:

**Q1. Grounds mechanics.** Shall the evidence-delta check be a deterministic predicate over claim fields evaluated by transport, or a prose constraint interpreted by the objector? Which failure is cheaper: rigidity or glibness?

**Q2. Pair falsifiability.** A pair that never disagrees is capitulation theater with tenure. What pair-level falsifier can detect this without dissolving the duo mechanism itself?

**Q3. Occipital fit and the lateralization rule.** The content-versus-geometry split is the weakest lobe mapping, and its left/right assignment (focal content decoding versus gestalt geometry) is correspondingly the most exposed application of the §5.2 rule. Defend it, revise it, or name the ninth role that replaces it. More broadly: does the uniform focal-left / panoramic-right rule hold for all four lobes, or does any lobe demand an inversion?

**Q4. Consolidation trigger.** Shall Chronicle→Canon distillation run on schedule, on volume, on idle detection, or a conjunction thereof?

**Q5. Slot-model assignment.** With paid dispatch demoted to emergency rations, shall paired lenses run on two local backbones, one backbone with divergent context slices, or one backbone with divergent personae — and is the §5.4 ladder (lateralized instruction, then slices, then backbones) correctly ordered as the cheapest-first enforcement of Law 3? Note the tension this question poses between cost preference and decorrelation.

**Q6. The eight-role count.** The count is now presented as arithmetic — the unique product of four pipeline stages (§5.1) and two obligatory witnesses per stage (§5.2). Does this derivation discharge the original objection that the count was anatomically generated rather than workload-derived? What minimum viable organism does actual workload evidence justify — eight, fewer, or another lattice entirely?

**Q7. Occupancy legality.** With authority and funding now orthogonal, which combinations of role-kind and slot-kind are lawful promotion states, which are tumors, and which are formally impossible?

**Q8. Tenant identity.** When a slot dies and its role re-funds with a different model, is the successor the same agent? Attribution, decorrelation history, and falsifier ledgers all presume an answer the ontology has not stated. Is hemispheric identity (left-of-its-lobe, right-of-its-lobe) a stable attribution key across generations?

**Q9. Router credit assignment.** The macro router lacks a stated learning signal and risks ossifying into a hand-maintained table — the roster-drift lesion reborn. What reward signal keeps it adaptive without violating Law 5?

**Q10. Consolidation gating.** Are distilled `convention_claims` subject to the objection loop? Ungated sleep-learning installs dreams as doctrine; gating breaks the pineal's offline guarantee.

**Q11. Interoception schema.** Which literal formulas over the cost ledger implement appetite, satiety, and throttle such that the endocrine tier becomes executable rather than evocative?

**Q12. Sub-selector scoring.** Which dispute-local features — concession slope, evidence-delta record, position volatility, prior jerk history — shall the embedded circuit score, with what weights, and what symmetric control arm shall the epoch-5 falsifier grade the hardening variant against?
