**Q7 — Split-brain drift detection.**

**Q10 — Interoception formulas.**
- **Formulas (over the cost ledger, all windows W = rolling 60 minutes, EWMA with α=0.1):**
  - Ledger primitives: `spend(t)` cumulative USD-normalized burn (local inference metered at marginal electricity rate; paid dispatch at invoice rate), `tokens(t)`, `queue_depth(t)`, `budget_B` (operator-recorded), `quality proxy q(t)` — *the Law 1 amendment rides here*: q = golden-set-subsampled correctness on a fixed probe battery (N≥20 probes/hour, sampled by medullary scheduler, never by a deliberator — Law 5 intact).

  - **Satiety** `S = ewma(q)·(1 + ewma(Δspend)/(ewma(Δtokens)+ε))⁻¹` — quality achieved per dollar, velocity-damped. High satiety (>0.7) permits consolidation scheduling (Q11 trigger co-input) and residency demotion.

- **Failure semantics:** True-positive throttle (genuine overspend): latency rises, spend decays — benign by construction. - **Falsifier (flat-rate null hypothesis):** All three formulas must beat a static flat throttle (fixed 20% dispatch delay) and fixed-batch scheduling on joint objective `ewma(q)/ewma(spend)` over a two-week operating epoch. Flat rate is the strongest null precisely because it is free; if hormonal control cannot beat free, the entire endocrine tier — hypothalamus, pituitary, pineal pacemaking — dies by Law 4.
