**Cost shape:** The rigidity tax falls on legitimate-but-nonstandard evidence (a visual proof, a log excerpt without a line-numbered citation format) — a genuine cost, paid in false terminations. If predicate-mode false-terminates (kills a dispute that predicate-mode's own escalation record shows was later resolved correctly by the operator at low cost) at a rate materially higher than prose-mode's capitulation rate (flip without new evidence, per flip-rate tracking in §9 10.1), predicate-first is falsified *for that evidence class*, not in general — relax per evidence class, never per slot.

**And the mis-scoping, stated:** the form asks *which mechanism*, but the Green Paper's §8 and Law 5 leave open *which organ* adjudicates. Deterministic predicates can be evaluated by substrate (Law 5-clean). ### Q2 — Pair falsifiability
**Proposed pair-level metric:** **Calibrated disagreement with correctness attribution** — measured on resolved golden-set items, not live disputes. For each lobe-pair: (a) disagreement rate on items where the item's ground truth is known; (b) conditional correctness: P(pair-correct | disagreed) versus P(pair-correct | agreed).

**Mechanism:** Two-sided band, symmetric trigger on violation:
- **Floor violation** (disagreement ≈ 0 across the golden set): split-brain drift / capitulation theater with tenure → mandatory promotion up the §5.4 decorrelation ladder *at the lobe's expense*: the ladder is climbed on the lobe's own metabolism, giving the lobe skin in the game.
- **Ceiling violation** (disagreement high, but P(correct | disagreed) ≈ chance): the pair manufactures dissent for show — decorrelation theater, the mirror pathology, and one the v1.2 text does not name. The pair is demoted one ladder rung (converging instructions) *or* brought before the operator for divorce/unfunding review.

**Cost shape:** Measurement runs on resolved items only, so its cost amortizes into the golden-set runs the epoch evaluation (§11) already requires. Enforcement is the expensive arm: forced ladder-climbing buys backbone divergence the workload may not have justified. Mitigation: promotions triggered by floor-violations are funded from the epoch budget, not the lobe's, with the lobe paying only the marginal delta.

**Falsifier:** The metric itself can misfire in both directions. False floor-violation: a lobe whose task class is genuinely easy (e.g., an over-populated circuit doing deterministic triage — see Q6) shows low disagreement *correctly*. Guard: floor evaluated per lobe, adjusted for item difficulty distribution, never global. False ceiling-violation: a pair correctly disagreeing on genuinely hard items shows high disagreement *and* low conditional correctness. Guard: the trigger requires the pair to be wrong-together on resolved items — correlation of *errors*, not mere disagreement, is the signature that distinguishes theater from honest difficulty.

### Q10 — Consolidation gating
**Position:** Ungated **at write-time**; gated **at promotion-to-doctrine time** — a two-phase gate that respects both constraints the dilemma poses as exclusive, but are not.

**Mechanism:** Pineal distills freely during sleep: every candidate `convention_claim` enters a **probation state** on the blackboard, marked provisional, and is *usable* (retrievable, citable in disputes as supporting material) but not *authoritative* (cannot serve as an objection's stopping ground, cannot terminate an evidence-delta loop on citation alone). Probationary doctrine accumulates citations — each use in a dispute or task is logged. A candidate crosses to authoritative when its citation-supported usage record clears a Law-4-grade bar (used successfully N times without a failed dispute against it). The pineal's offline guarantee is fully preserved — no live dispute ever suspends distillation; the gating happens at canonization, not at generation.

**How this answers the dilemma's horns:** Ungated *generation* preserves sleep; gated *promotion* prevents "dreams as doctrine" — a candidate that exists is not a claim that binds. The paper's own ontology supplies the intermediate state (probationary claims are claims-in-paper — Law 4's derivation-order clause from the Foundational Theory §1: "roles exist freely on paper; slots must justify themselves" — applied to doctrine). The three-way import is the same law, applied at doctrine level.

**Faisifier:** Null hypothesis: no probation tier (candidates enter Canon authoritative on distillation). Falsify the two-phase gate if probationary overhead (logging, dispute-time lookup of provisional status) costs more than the bad-doctrine incidents it prevents, measured on the golden set over ≥ K distillation cycles. The bad-doctrine rate itself is measurable: a `convention_claim` that is later contradicted by Chronicle fact is the incident class; count them per arm.

### Q11 — Interoception schema
**Proposed formulas — the actual math, as the form demands:**

Windows and notation: sliding window W (tasks), velocity window w << W. All quantities from the cost ledger. Constants k₁, k₂ are set by operator policy; defaults proposed below.

- **Appetite (warning):** `spend_runway_ratio(t) = spend(W)/runway_provisioned(W)`; appetite fires when `spend_runway_ratio > k₁ ⋅ baseline(W₀)` where W₀ is the epoch's opening window. k₁ default 1.25.
- **Satiety (confirmation):** fires when `runway_remaining(t) / planned_tasks_remaining(t) ≥ k₂ ⋅ runway_per_task(W₀)`, k₂ default 1.1 — i.e., per-task metabolism at or under the epoch's opening rate, with headroom.
- **Throttle (gentle action):** scalar `T(t) = σ(k₃ ⋅ (spend_velocity(w) − velocity_baseline(W)))`, sigmoid-bounded in [0,1]; the hypothalamus multiplies suggested batch sizes and concurrency hints by (1 − T/2). Soft signal, never a halt — the form's "failure semantics" demand it be non-binding; Law 5 already guarantees it.

**Failure semantics:**
- True-positive (genuinely hot): throttle nudges batch sizes down, dispatch queues lengthen modestly; recovery to baseline within W restores T → 0 smoothly — no cliff, no argument, no operator attention demanded.
- False-positive (benign spike — a legitimately expensive single task inflates velocity): throttle slows subsequent tasks unnecessarily; the *sensitivity* of σ to w-size controls this — w sized to task-class noise, not global noise.
- The detection power of these formulas is admittedly modest (residual-surprise thresholds are stronger, per the earlier review's E1), but these are the *implementable floor*: every constant is a policy dial the operator can defend, no learning, no interpretation, full Law 5 cleanliness.

**Falsifier:** the form's named null — flat-rate throttle — plus a third arm: the E1-style precision-weighted residual (burn-rate prediction error thresholded). The implementable floor must beat flat-rate on (cost overruns prevented / operator interruptions) per epoch. If residual-thresholding beats the implementable floor on the same metric, the floor is upgraded — no loser, only ordering. This is the rare consultation answer that is *entirely falsifiable per-operator*, because the inputs are the operator's own ledger.

### Q12 — Sub-selector scoring
**Position:** The **feature set is sound; the triggering mechanism is the breach** — and this response's finding is blocking, not advisory.

**Mechanism (proposed feature set and rough weighting):** concession-slope (weight ~0.4), evidence-delta-record (weight ~0.3), position-volatility (weight ~0.2), prior-jerk-history (weight ~0.1), seeded roll as tie-break — exactly the four features the paper names, with weights admitting they are priors, not measurements; the epoch-5 falsifier exists to retire them.

**The blocking finding — Law 5 breach as drafted:** The Green Paper's §8 says the jerk fires on detecting "stalled convergence — positions not actually moving, evidence-deltas thinning, the penultimate round arriving without resolution." Computing "positions not actually moving" and "evidence-deltas thinning" requires infrastructure to **semantically interpret dispute content** — exactly what Law 5 forbids ("infrastructure never deliberates … any component empowered to arbitrate a contested question is a judge organ"). The paper cannot have both: either the LC triggers are mechanical (round-count thresholds, citation-field-presence booleans, embedding-distance between successive positions — a *number computed*, which can be ruled computation rather than interpretation, if the paper rules it so explicitly), or some substrate component is an unlicensed interpreter of contested content. The Foundational Theory's v1.4 noticed this class of problem in passing (its §7.2 says the jerk is "deterministic in implementation"); the Green Paper *strengthened the trigger's semantic richness* ("genuinely new evidence," "thinning") without noticing it widened the breach. **Resolution the paper must rule on before blueprint:** restrict LC inputs to mechanically computable features — (a) round index ≥ penultimate, (b) citation-presence flags from claim fields (substrate-known), (c) embedding-distance between successive position statements < ε (a deterministic similarity computation, ruled a measurement by explicit clause), (d) seeded roll. Nothing requiring reading the *meaning* of an objection. This preserves the organ's function at the cost of one explicit ruling sentence. Without that ruling, Law 5 and §8 cannot both stand, and the paper's internal-compliance claim (§2, "we name in advance") is falsified by its own §8.

**Cost shape:** mechanical-feature restriction slightly delays jerk firing (embedding distance is a cruder stall-detector than true semantic "not actually moving") — a bounded latency tax on a rare event. Unrestricted triggers have zero latency cost and an unbounded constitutional cost. Same shape as Q1: bounded countable failure vs. unbounded silent corruption.

**Bias test beyond same-party-targeting:** **cross-dispute targeting symmetry** — over the golden set, the LC should select each hemispheric *disposition* (approach vs. wary lenses) at rates proportional to their representation in disputes. If hardening orders systematically land on the *right* lens (wary lenses) more than proportionally, the sub-selector is disposition-biased — and since right-lens stances are structurally non-accommodating, the bias manufactures systematically harder, longer disputes, compounding the jerk's selection pressure with the lenses' dispositional priors. A disposition-blind LC is testable as a null: shuffled party assignment (preserving dispute content, swapping party labels) should not shift targeting. If it does, bias in who gets hardened is bias in dispute outcomes — the paper's own stated fear, and a sharper detector than the party-repetition test alone.

**Control arm for the epoch-5 falsifier:** the mirror control stands — at equal budget, "forced concession-or-maximal-specificity" against the hardening order. If the mirror resolves disputes at the same rate and cost, the hardening variant is halving the LC machinery for free quality: Law 4 deletes the reflex rather than the guard. A second control arm, cheaper: **shortened-bound-only** (the jerk's null hypothesis from the Foundational Theory §7.2) — if a mechanically-triggered jerk beats shortened-bound-only on cost-per-correct-resolution, the reflex earns its metabolism; if not, Law 4 fires on the whole organ. Both controls together give the reflex two independent ways to die, which is the correct number of ways to die for a brand-new reflex.

**D2. Missing pathologies — with their theorems, as the form demands:**

1. **Metric capture** *(the Q9 finding, stated as a pathology so the enumeration is complete).* **Theorem:** any organ whose funding key (Law 4) or fitness metric (Law 1) is a *computed proxy* for the organism's mission is optimizable independently of the mission — and under Law 4's persistent selection pressure, *will be* optimized independently of the mission given sufficient epochs. Manifestations: router reward-gaming (Q9), distillation-volume maximization (the pineal optimizing claim-count instead of claim-value — directly enabled if Q4/Q10's triggers are volume-based), pair disagreement-rate farming (Q2's ceiling violation, enshrined as a survival strategy). Guard: mission-anchored audits — periodic golden-set runs where the proxy (cost-quality delta, disagreement rate) is scored against ground truth, so the gap between proxy and mission is itself measured. Not in the paper's pathology list; forecastable from the paper's own laws; blocking for blueprint, advisory for green-paper stage.
2. **Homology capture** *(named in honor of the paper's own method).* **Theorem:** metaphor-as-specification (§2) imports behavioral expectations from the metaphor's referent; under sustained operator familiarity, the *referent's* behaviors become arguments — "the basal ganglia does X in rats, therefore the router should X" — and the architecture inherits biological constraints it never chose, at the cost of engineering alternatives. This is metaphor worship's subtler sibling: 10.10 catches funding-by-name; it does not catch *reasoning-by-referent*. Note the irony guard: this pathology applies to the reviewer's own Q3 response (which argued an inversion *from* the lateralization literature) as much as to the paper. Guard: a standing distinction — referent behavior may *motivate* a design, never *justify* one; justification is always golden-set-falsifiable. The paper half-has this ("biological warrant is generative, not evidentiary," §5.4); it should be promoted from §5.4-caution to §2-doctrine.
3. **Escalation starvation** *(M5 from the earlier review, restated in the paper's own vocabulary so it belongs in its ledger).* **Theorem:** the organism's appeal path (§6 rule 5, §8) is a resource with finite capacity — the operator — and every design decision that lowers the cost of escalating (hard bounds, jerk rounds, mandatory post-jerk escalation) increases arrival rate at a fixed-capacity sink. Under load, the queue *before* the operator becomes the organism's dominant latency and cost term. This is distinct from 10.8 (undirected escalation), which polices *paid dispatch* without record — it does not police *operator attention* exhaustion, which costs no dispatch at all. Guard: an escalation-rate vital sign (escalations per completed task per epoch, budget-alarmed at the hypothalamic level), symmetric to the callosal-traffic vital sign §4 already grants the corpus callosum. The paper gives callosal traffic a vital sign and the appeal path none; the asymmetry is itself evidence the pathology was invisible at drafting time.
